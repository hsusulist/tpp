Before you play

This game deals with the death of a parent, grief, memory loss, alcohol abuse, family conflict, non-graphic violence and a house fire. It is intended for players aged 13+.

If any of these topics are hard for you right now, it's okay to come back later.

How to play

3 AM on your parents' wedding anniversary, Dad wakes you up and asks you to clean the house. He leaves at 6:00 AM and comes back at 5:00 PM.

WASD move · Shift run · Mouse look
E / Left click interact · Hold LMB + move mouse scrub
1–3 / Mouse wheel select inventory slot · G drop · Right click / F throw
M map · Tab progress · P photo pieces · Esc pause
  • You can carry 3 things at once — tools take a slot too. Get the rag, broom, mop and trash bags from the Storage room at the end of the hallway.
  • There is no clock on screen. Check the grandfather clock in the hallway (or the kitchen clock) to see the time.
  • Drop or throw things — a throw that lands in a trash bin or the laundry basket counts! Press E at a bin to dump everything you carry into it.
  • Pick up clutter and carry it where it belongs: books on the shelf, clothes in the laundry basket, mail in the foyer tray, dishes in the sink...
  • The Trash Bag holds 10 pieces of trash. The kitchen bin fills up — take its bag to the bins outside (kitchen back door).
  • Wipe dust with the Rag, push dirt away with the Broom, scrub stains with the Mop.
  • You can't do everything in time. Prioritise!
  • Clean half the kitchen to find Mom's cake recipe. Don't leave it in the oven too long...
  • There are 21 endings. After 7 different ones, a secret is revealed.

Settings

Tip: sleeping in your own bed skips to 5 PM.
